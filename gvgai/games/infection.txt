BasicGame
    SpriteSet
        sword > Flicker color=BLACK limit=5 singleton=True
        entrance > SpawnPoint total=5 color=PINK cooldown=100 stype=doctor portal=True
        virus > Immovable color=RED 

        normal   > ShootAvatar stype=sword color=WHITE 
        carrier  > ShootAvatar stype=sword color=BROWN  frameRate=8
        host     > RandomNPC speed=0.25  cons=1 color=GREEN 
        infected > RandomNPC speed=0.25  cons=1 color=ORANGE speed=0.5  frameRate=8
        doctor > RandomNPC speed=0.25  cons=1 color=BLUE speed=0.2 
        wall > Immovable color=GRAY  autotiling=True

    TerminationSet
        SpriteCounter stype=host   win=True

    InteractionSet
        doctor sword    > killSprite 
	doctor sword  > changeScore value=2
    
        normal wall       > stepBack
        carrier wall       > stepBack
        host  wall       > stepBack
        infected wall       > stepBack
        doctor wall       > stepBack


        carrier doctor  > transformTo stype=normal  
	carrier doctor > changeScore value=-1
        host carrier      > transformTo stype=infected 
	host carrier > changeScore value=2
        infected doctor > transformTo stype=host
        normal infected > transformTo stype=carrier 
	normal virus  > transformTo stype=carrier
        host infected > transformTo stype=infected 
	host virus    > transformTo stype=infected
        doctor virus    > killSprite

    LevelMapping
        1 >  doctor
        0 >  host
        x >  virus
        A >  normal
        e >  entrance
