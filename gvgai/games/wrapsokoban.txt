BasicGame

    SpriteSet
        ground > Immovable color=LIGHTGRAY
        hole   > Immovable color=BLUE
        avatar > OrientedAvatar rotateInPlace=False color=DARKBLUE
        bbox > Passive
            box    >  color=BROWN
            boxin  >  color=GOLD
        wall > Immovable color=DARKGRAY

    LevelMapping
        A > avatar ground
        o > hole
        * > box ground
        . > ground
        w > ground wall

    InteractionSet
        avatar EOS > wrapAround
        bbox avatar  > bounceForward
        box EOS > wrapAround
        avatar wall > stepBack
        bbox bbox   > undoAll
        bbox wall   > undoAll
        box hole    > transformTo stype=boxin
        box hole    > changeScore value=1
        boxin ground > transformTo stype=box
        boxin ground > changeScore value=1

    TerminationSet
        SpriteCounter stype=box    limit=0 win=True
