BasicGame
    SpriteSet
        floor > Immovable img=sprites/grass.png
        cocoon > Immovable color=GREEN img=sprites/cocoonb.png
        avatar    > MovingAvatar color=DARKBLUE img=sprites/angel.png
        butterfly > RandomNPC speed=0.6 color=PINK img=sprites/butterfly.png
        wall > Immovable color=DARKGRAY img=sprites/tree.png

    TerminationSet
        SpriteCounter stype=butterfly win=True bonus=10
        SpriteCounter stype=cocoon    win=False
        Timeout limit=2000 win=False

    InteractionSet
        avatar    wall   > stepBack
        butterfly wall > stepBack
        butterfly avatar > changeScore value=2
        butterfly avatar > killSprite 
        butterfly cocoon > cloneSprite
        butterfly butterfly > nothing
        avatar cocoon > nothing
        butterfly wall > nothing
        cocoon butterfly > killSprite

    LevelMapping
        . > floor 
        1 > floor butterfly
        0 > floor cocoon
        w > floor wall
        A > floor avatar
